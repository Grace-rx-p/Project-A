[ Zephyr Bloom ]

Scientific Name:

Flos zephyris metamorphosis

Discovered by / at:

Discovered by Grace in a quiet valley during a windy evening.

Family:

Flower-Butterfly Family. Scientists initially thought this was an exquisite plant pollinated by gentle breezes. Later, they discovered it is actually a symbiotic life form composed of a colony of butterflies in a “dormant crystalline state”.

Habitat:

Lives in alpine meadows and misty dawn forests with persistent soft winds. Prefers open natural spaces with moving air and changing light and shadow.

Appearance:

Ordinarily, it appears as an elegant, semi-transparent, slender flower glowing with faint light. Its flexible stem sways gently in the breeze. Upon closer inspection, however, the edges of its "petals" reveal extremely fine textures resembling insect wings.

Key Characteristics:

  • Sways gently with natural wind currents.
  • Senses nearby movement and human presence.
  • Metamorphoses petals into butterflies upon touch.
  • The swarm of butterflies drifts away with the wind, and the flower will then regenerate anew.

What we know so far about Zephyr Bloom ? After observation, we discovered Zephyr Bloom is a rare camouflaged creature. It takes the form of a flower to absorb nutrients from the wind. Upon contact, its petals transform into fluttering butterflies, regrouping into a new bloom once surroundings return to calm.
Move your cursor near Zephyr Bloom to disturb its surrounding airflow, causing its stem to lean nervously. Click directly on the creature to wake it up: watch its petals break away, transforming into butterflies that dance along the wind field across your screen!
